# Merchant logo sources

Downloaded on 2026-09-12. All artwork is bundled locally; rendering does not contact the source hosts or send transaction names to them.

- `zara.svg`, `mcdonalds.svg`, `google.svg`, `lidl.svg`, `ikea.svg`, `netflix.svg`, `spotify.svg`: unmodified [Simple Icons SVGs](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/develop/icons), downloaded from `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/develop/icons/{name}.svg`. Simple Icons is distributed under [CC0-1.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/LICENSE.md).
- `openai.svg`: unmodified [Simple Icons 14.15.0 SVG](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/14.15.0/icons/openai.svg), under [CC0-1.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/14.15.0/LICENSE.md).
- `wolt.svg`: authentic inline wordmark from the [official Wolt homepage](https://wolt.com/en). The original path is unchanged; its viewport is fitted to a centered 24 × 24 canvas and the page-specific CSS class is removed. Wolt retains the rights to its logo; no open-source license is asserted for this asset.
- `googleone.svg`: unmodified full-color SVG from [Google's official asset host](https://ssl.gstatic.com/subscriptions/management/home/logo_one_48dp_1147f17d6d2f67ab3a9ce3c55600631b.svg). Google retains the rights to its logo; no open-source license is asserted for this asset. Render this SVG as an image, rather than a monochrome CSS mask, to preserve its colors.

Logos identify merchants in transaction history. Brand names and logos remain trademarks of their respective owners; the source licenses do not grant trademark rights or imply endorsement.
